Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Make Ten is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your information.

Information we collect

None. Make Ten does not collect, store, or send any personal information to the developer or to anyone else. The app does not require an account and works without an internet connection.

Information saved on your device

To keep your progress, the app saves a few things only on your phone:

  • The answers you found and the hints you used for each day
  • Your streak and stats, which are worked out from those saved days
  • Your settings, such as the running total option
  • Whether you have seen the rules, and whether the Puzzle Archive is unlocked

This information never leaves your device, and the developer cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widget only shows today's digits, which it works out from the date, and it does not read your saved progress.

Purchases

If you buy the Puzzle Archive, the payment is processed entirely by Apple. The developer does not receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. Buying or restoring is the only time the app connects to the internet, and it connects only to the App Store. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Make Ten contains no analytics tools, no advertising, no third-party code, and no tracking of any kind.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app creates a short line of plain text, like your puzzle number and score. It is passed to the share sheet on your phone, and you choose where it goes. The developer does not receive it.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
